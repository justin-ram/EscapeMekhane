Vista Bundled Biome Templates
=============================

This folder holds the Biome Templates that Vista ships with, the ones shown in the
Wizard's "Create Biome" gallery. They are shared across all editions (Personal, Indie,
Pro).

Please do not edit, move, rename, or delete anything in this folder, unless you are
deliberately replacing the bundled set and accept that your changes will be overwritten
the next time Vista updates. Edits here are not preserved across updates, and a broken
or missing template can break the gallery.

Want your own templates? You do not need to touch this folder. Create a Biome Template
anywhere else in your project (Assets > Create > Vista > Biome Template) and it shows up
in the gallery automatically. The Wizard discovers every valid Biome Template in the
project, wherever it lives.
